Privacy Policy

Tampa Loop. Last updated September 7, 2026.

Tampa Loop is a weekly Tampa Bay events newsletter published by KLC Endeavors LLC, doing business as Tampa Loop ("Tampa Loop," "we," "us"). This policy covers the Tampa Loop newsletter, its website, and the sponsor quote pages we host, including the Bright Lights Tampa quote page.

What we collect

  • Newsletter subscribers: your email address, and if you choose to answer our short survey, your content interests, who you plan weekends for, and the part of Tampa Bay you live in. Our newsletter platform, beehiiv, also records standard email engagement (opens and clicks) and the source you signed up from.
  • Sponsor quote requests: if you fill out a quote form on a sponsor page, we collect the details you enter: name, phone number, email address, zip code, preferred install window, and the type of lighting you are interested in, along with the newsletter link that brought you to the page.
  • Calls to a tracking number: if you call a phone number printed in the newsletter, the call is forwarded to the sponsor and we record the caller's number, the time, and the duration. Calls are not recorded.

How we use it

  • To send you the newsletter you subscribed to and to tailor its content.
  • To pass your quote request to the sponsor you asked to hear from, so they can contact you about the service you requested.
  • To measure, in aggregate, how many readers requested a quote from a sponsor. Reporting is aggregate only; no individual details appear in it.

Sharing

Quote request details are shared only with the sponsor named on the page you submitted, for the purpose of responding to your request. We do not sell your information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use-case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We use service providers to run the newsletter and the quote pages: beehiiv (email), Google (spreadsheets and email), Zapier (form delivery), GitHub (page hosting), and Twilio (call forwarding). Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

Text messages

Tampa Loop sends text messages only to business owners who have expressly opted in to lead notification alerts. Text alerts are optional: every advertiser receives lead alerts by email, and choosing not to receive texts has no effect on advertising with Tampa Loop or on any other service. We do not send text messages to newsletter subscribers or to people who request quotes. See the Terms and Conditions for the text message program terms.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link at the bottom of every issue.
  • Ask us to delete your quote request details by emailing kevincole400@gmail.com. We will also pass the request to the sponsor.
  • Text message recipients can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Retention

Quote request details are kept for the duration of the sponsor campaign and for reporting through the end of the following January, then deleted from our records. The sponsor keeps what you shared with them under their own policy.

Children

Tampa Loop is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Contact

Questions about this policy: kevincole400@gmail.com.
